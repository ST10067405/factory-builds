Home Security, Plainly Explained

Straightforward advice for Queensburgh and Escombe homeowners — what to do when you're locked out, how to choose hardware, and what's worth knowing before your next call-out.

Common questions

What should I do first if I'm locked out?

Stay calm, check every door and window before calling anyone (a back or side entry is sometimes open), and avoid forcing the lock yourself — that usually turns a simple call-out into a full lock replacement. Then call or WhatsApp a locksmith and wait somewhere safe.

Should I rekey my lock or replace it?

Rekeying changes the internal pins so old keys stop working, without swapping the hardware — cheaper and faster, and enough after a move-in or lost key. Replacement is worth it if the lock itself is old, damaged, or you want to upgrade the security grade.

What should I look for in a front door lock?

A solid deadbolt in addition to the handle-set lock, a strike plate with long screws into the door frame (not just the trim), and hardware rated for security rather than just style. A locksmith can talk you through what suits your specific door.
