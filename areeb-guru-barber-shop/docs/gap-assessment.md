## Where we already win

- WhatsApp is pushed in four places (nav, hero, floating button, Visit section) — none of the three competitors match that; only Cam's has a single WhatsApp link, the other two have none at all.
- We're the only one of the four with any real content — a 6-article grooming Journal with schema-marked FAQ. None of the three competitors run a blog or journal.
- We're the only site with a Maps embed *and* a one-tap "Navigate" link on the page itself.
- We're the lightest page by a wide margin (~20KB vs. 142KB–779KB) — a real advantage for a walk-in shop's mobile-data customers.
- We already lead with the 4.5★/52-review social proof in the hero, not buried lower on the page.

## Where competitors beat us today

- All three show real prices somewhere on their site or booking flow; ours still shows "R —" placeholders across the whole services list.
- Cam's and Legends both have working online booking; ours is call/WhatsApp only.
- Cam's and Legends both use 100% real shop photography; ours is mostly disclosed stock (1 of 5 gallery images is real).
- Cam's has real, quoted, star-rated testimonials; ours only states a rating number with no individual reviews yet.

## What closes the gap

- **Booking/calendar integration — R3,600.** Matches what Cam's and Legends already offer; turns "call or WhatsApp only" into a same-tier feature.
- **Copywriting — R850/page.** Converts the placeholder "R —" services list and rating stat into a real priced menu plus quoted testimonials, once the client supplies both.
- **WhatsApp automation — R6,500 setup.** Extends our existing WhatsApp lead further than any competitor has gone — auto-replies keep the channel live even when no one's near a phone.
