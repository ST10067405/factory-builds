The Workshop Notes

Plain-language car care for Hillary and Queensburgh drivers — no jargon, no upsell. Six practical guides on the things that actually go wrong.

Common questions

What does DZL Services and Repairs actually work on?

General mechanical work on everyday cars — servicing, diagnostics, brakes, clutch, suspension and both minor and major repairs. Message us with your vehicle's make and the issue and we'll confirm we can help.

Do I need to book, or can I just bring the car in?

Message us on WhatsApp first with the issue — it helps us plan the bay and give you a realistic idea of timing before you drop the car off.

How do you decide what a repair will cost?

We diagnose the actual fault first, then quote before any work starts, so you know roughly what you're paying for before it happens.

How do I book?
