## Where we already win
- Only one of the four with a live chatbot of any kind (theirs is zero, ours is a starting point).
- Only one that puts the 5.0/37 Google rating front and centre — the others bury real ratings on Tripadvisor, off their own site.
- Only one with a Journal built on structured FAQ schema out of the gate — a real AI-search advantage as-is.
- Lightest page weight of the four — genuinely faster to load on mobile than Chefs' Table or Hartford.
- Only one with an embedded Google Map at all — two competitors don't have one on-site.

## Where competitors beat us today
- Two of three (Chefs' Table, 9th Avenue) already have real online booking widgets — we have DM-only, which is our single biggest functional gap.
- Chefs' Table publishes actual menu prices; we currently show none, which is defensible for a changing tasting menu but still a diner objection.
- 9th Avenue carries 701 reviews at 4.5★ on Tripadvisor — far more social proof volume than our 37, even though they don't display it.
- All three appear to have larger, deeper photo galleries than our 5 vetted images.
- Hartford has full SEO fundamentals (meta + schema) done properly; our own homepage is still missing schema and is currently `noindex` as a draft.

## What closes the gap
- Real booking/calendar integration → **R3,600** (closes the single biggest functional gap vs. 2 of 3 competitors).
- AI chatbot upgrade → **R9,500 setup + R950 pm** (turns our lead into a durable advantage instead of a placeholder).
- Photography/video shoot (TBC, scope separately) → grows the gallery past 5 images to match or beat competitor depth.
- SEO Visibility+GEO → **R8,500 pm** (adds schema/structured data site-wide and targets "best fine dining Durban" queries where Chefs' Table and 9th Avenue already rank).
