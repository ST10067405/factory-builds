## Our recommendation between the two — The Foundation, R16,500

**The Foundation (≤5 pages, motion, WhatsApp, Maps, basic SEO, hosting + domain handled, 2 revisions).**

Seven Seeds is a straightforward local eatery: one location, no franchise complexity, no booking system, no e-commerce. The demo already proves out a Foundation-scope site (home + journal, Maps embed, mobile-first build). The one thing the package must emphasise is the **menu** — right now every price on the demo reads "R —" because we have no real menu to work from. Confirming a real menu with prices is the single biggest gap between "concept" and "launchable," so we'd scope the Foundation build to prioritise that page.

The Structure (R32,000) isn't justified here — there's no booking depth, gallery/testimonial system, or analytics need that a coffee shop with a simple menu and one location requires.

## Worth adding

- **Newsletter setup — R2,800.** Café regulars are a repeat-visit audience; a simple sign-up (specials, new menu items) suits this business better than most.
- **Extra page (QR-code printable menu) — R1,400, only if needed.** If they want a standalone page designed to be scanned via a table QR code (separate from the on-site menu section), this is the add-on. Skip it if the on-site menu section is enough.
- **WhatsApp automation — R6,500 setup, once they have a business number.** Not orderable today — see the phone-number gap below. Flag it as the natural next step once that's solved.
- **Copywriting — R850/page.** Their menu, hours and "our story" content don't exist yet in written form; if they can't supply finished copy, this covers writing it up from what they tell us.

## Keeping it alive

- **Care plan: Essential, R450/pm.** Nothing here suggests frequent monthly content churn (no rotating specials system, no multi-location updates) — Essential covers routine upkeep without over-selling.
- **SEO: Launch setup, R4,500 once-off.** Worth doing once, to get found for "coffee shop Pinetown" — there's real local competition (Madeleines, Bellevue Café, Springside Café all rank with live sites — see competitors.md) but not enough paid-search intensity in this category to justify an ongoing Visibility retainer. Once-off Launch is the right size.
