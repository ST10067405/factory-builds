## Where we already win

- We're the only one of the four with an embedded Google Map and directions link on the homepage — all three competitors make you leave the site to find them.
- We're the only one with any content/journal system — 6 articles plus FAQPage schema markup already in the code. None of the three competitors run a blog.
- We're the only one with an on-site chat widget answering the obvious questions (location, menu, hours, ordering) without leaving the page.
- Our page is lighter and simpler than any of the three WordPress builds — faster first paint, no plugin bloat.
- We display the real, current Google rating (4.4★/121) directly on the page; only Madeleines has an equivalent live review signal (via a widget).

## Where competitors beat us today

- All three have real photography of their actual food, drinks and space. Ours is 100% stock, clearly disclosed as such.
- All three have a working WhatsApp contact button. We can't add one yet — Seven Seeds has no verified phone number.
- All three are live, indexed, real websites today. Ours is a draft (`noindex`), not yet public.
- Madeleines runs an active review widget pulling in ongoing social proof; ours is a static number.

## What closes the gap

1. **Real photography** — once the client supplies photos (or we arrange a shoot), swap out all 4 stock images. Bundled into The Foundation build; add copywriting (R850/page) if captions/descriptions are also needed.
2. **A business phone number, then WhatsApp automation (R6,500 setup)** — closes the one gap all three competitors already have on us.
3. **A real, priced menu** — copywriting R850/page (or R3,500 for up to 5 pages) to turn the client's actual menu into finished copy, replacing the "R —" placeholders.
4. **Ongoing review visibility** — worth a mention, not a default recommendation: Professional care (R1,200/pm) would support rotating testimonial content if the client wants that later; Essential is the right starting tier for now.
