Three real, independent coffee shops/cafés in the same catchment (Upper Highway corridor / greater Durban, all within ~15 min of Pinetown), each with a working website (verified HTTP 200). One chain (Xpresso Café, also on Josiah Gumede Rd, Pinetown) was found and ruled out in favour of independents, since locals weren't scarce.

## Madeleines
- **Town:** Hillcrest & Kloof
- **URL:** https://madeleines.co.za/ (verified 200)
- **Site:** WordPress/Elementor build. Real photography throughout (18 uploaded images on the homepage), a dedicated `/menus/` page, an Elfsight-powered review widget, and a reCAPTCHA-protected contact form. Polished, content-rich, positioned as "your neighbourhood spot" for coffee, wholesome food and artisanal bakes.

## Bellevue Café
- **Town:** Kloof & Ballito
- **URL:** https://bellevuecafe.co.za/ (verified 200)
- **Site:** Two-location site with separate menu pages per branch (`/menus-kloof/`, `/menus-ballito/`), WhatsApp contact, reservation/booking language, wood-fired pizza + artisanal coffee positioning. Heavier WordPress build (~160KB homepage).

## Springside Café
- **Town:** Hillcrest (branded "Cafe Hillcrest")
- **URL:** https://springsidecafe.co.za/ (verified 200)
- **Site:** Warm, tranquil positioning — high teas, garden setting. Menu is a downloadable PDF rather than an on-page menu. WhatsApp contact present, "testimonial" content referenced. Lightest of the three builds (~105KB homepage).
