## Where we already win

- We're the only one of the four with an on-site FAQ chatbot that escalates to WhatsApp — none of the three competitors run any chat widget.
- We're the only one with a working content/journal system carrying schema markup — 6 full articles plus `FAQPage` structured data. Competitors either show no blog (New Images), or only ambiguous blog/journal references in their page code with no confirmed live articles (Synergy, Static).
- We're the only one that combines an embedded map *and* a live, honestly-disclosed Google rating (4.8★/35) on the page — Synergy has heavier review content but nothing as transparently sourced; New Images shows no rating signal at all.
- Our page is a single lightweight static file with no CMS/builder overhead — Synergy's homepage alone runs 340KB+ of builder markup.
- We already have both click-to-call and a WhatsApp-style CTA live in the hero and a floating button; Synergy has neither WhatsApp option on its site at all.

## Where competitors beat us today

- All three are live, real websites; ours is still a `noindex` draft with placeholder pricing, by design, pending Salon Ash Blonde's sign-off.
- Synergy and Static both lean on "book online"/"book" messaging — even without a confirmed live calendar, they read as more booking-ready than our phone/message-only demo.
- New Images runs a genuinely extensive real photo gallery across two locations; ours is 100% stock, honestly disclosed but still stock.
- New Images and Static both carry cleanly branded `<title>` tags start-to-finish; ours is draft-appropriate but not yet the final, go-live title.

## What closes the gap

1. **Real photography** — once Salon Ash Blonde supplies photos (or a shoot is arranged), swap out all 4 stock images. No extra fee for client-supplied photos; copywriting (R850/page) if captions are also wanted.
2. **A real, priced colour menu** — copywriting R850/page (or R3,500 for up to 5 pages) to turn the salon's actual price list into finished copy, replacing the "R —" placeholders — a gap two of the three competitors share with us today.
3. **Online booking/calendar integration — R3,600** — closes the "book online" gap that Synergy and Static already lean on in their messaging.
4. **Going live** — the fastest way to close the "we're still a draft" gap: The Foundation (R16,500) takes the site from `noindex` draft to a real, indexed, hosted site with its own domain.
