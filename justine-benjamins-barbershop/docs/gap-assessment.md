## Where we already win
- We're the only one of the four putting WhatsApp within a tap everywhere (nav, hero, floating button, footer) — matches how this shop's customers actually reach out, and none of the three competitors offer WhatsApp at all.
- We're the only one surfacing a real review count/rating (4.6★, 106 reviews) anywhere on the page — none of Xclusive, Nev or Stag 'n Dear display a star rating or review count.
- Our journal content is hyper-local (Durban humidity, fades, Upper Highway) rather than generic grooming content — a sharper angle than Nev's or Stag's more generic blog posts.
- Lightest, simplest page of the four (~20KB, no CMS/plugin overhead) — likely the fastest first paint on mobile data, which matters for a phone-first, walk-in audience.

## Where competitors beat us today
- Real, working booking exists on all three competitor sites; ours is still a placeholder ("coming soon").
- Xclusive Barber runs a genuine 10-photo real gallery and full LocalBusiness/HairSalon schema (address, geo, hours) — depth we don't have yet.
- Nev The Barber and Stag 'n Dear both publish real, itemised prices (R110–R290); our services list is entirely blank placeholders.
- Nev The Barber backs its site with a live chat widget (Crisp) — a human fallback our canned FAQ chips don't offer.

## What closes the gap
- **Booking/calendar integration — R3,600.** Matches all three competitors and removes the only visible "not live yet" note on our site.
- **Copywriting, 5 pages — R3,500.** Turns the placeholder price list into real, sellable copy once the client confirms their menu — closing the pricing gap against Nev and Stag 'n Dear.
- **SEO Launch setup — R4,500 once.** Adds proper on-page schema (LocalBusiness/HairSalon-equivalent), closing the structured-data gap against Xclusive Barber.
- **WhatsApp automation — R6,500 setup.** Converts our one clear structural advantage (WhatsApp everywhere) into an active differentiator none of the three competitors currently match.
