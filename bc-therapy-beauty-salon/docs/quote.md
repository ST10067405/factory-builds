## Our recommendation between the two — The Foundation, R16,500

BC Therapy is an owner-operated home studio (single therapist — "Bronwen" is in the Instagram handle), not a multi-room operation needing booking-system depth or franchise-scale analytics. The demo above already fits inside a ≤5-page site with motion, WhatsApp, Maps and basic SEO — which is exactly what the Foundation tier covers, plus hosting and domain handled and 2 rounds of revisions. This is the tier that takes what's already built and gets it live, not a bigger rebuild.
