## Where we already win

- Only one of the four with a confirmed floating WhatsApp button wired to a real, working mobile number and click-to-call together — L&T has neither, RvD's is unclear.
- The lightest, single-file build of the four — the fastest likely mobile load, where beauty-treatment searches happen overwhelmingly on the go.
- The only one with a written content hub (6-post Journal + FAQ schema) — none of the three peers show comparable localised written content (Durban humidity, aftercare guides).
- We display the 5.0★/37-review rating directly and prominently — a stronger, more visible number than anything surfaced on the three competitor homepages.

## Where competitors beat us today

- **Real photography.** All three competitors show real salon or work photography; ours is currently 100% stock (disclosed) after all 6 scouted real photos were rejected — two were of a different Arkatype client, the rest wrong-venue or unverifiable. This is the single biggest visible gap.
- **Booking depth.** L&T has full online booking plus e-commerce checkout, RvD books via Shedul; we currently offer call/WhatsApp only, with the site itself saying booking is "coming soon."
- **Visible pricing.** L&T shows extensive on-page pricing; ours shows "R —" placeholders throughout.
- **Structured data & indexability.** L&T ships two JSON-LD schema blocks on its homepage; ours carries none there (FAQ schema lives only on the Journal subpage), and the site is still flagged `noindex` as a draft — so right now we're not discoverable in search at all, while all three competitors are live and indexed today.

## What closes the gap

1. **Real photography, as a hard requirement of the Foundation build (R16,500).** Replace 100% stock with a real shoot of the studio and finished work — the client's own top ask, and the biggest visible gap against all three competitors.
2. **Booking integration — R3,600.** Brings Thul's to parity with L&T (Shopify checkout) and RvD (Shedul), replacing "coming soon" with a real booking flow.
3. **SEO Launch — R4,500 once.** Add LocalBusiness/Service schema to the homepage and remove `noindex` at launch — closes the structured-data gap with L&T and makes the site indexable at all.
4. **Copywriting for the service menu — R850/page (5 pages R3,500).** Once pricing is confirmed, replace "R —" placeholders to match L&T's visible-pricing advantage.
