## Where we already win

- **Built-in FAQ chat widget** — none of the three competitors show any on-page Q&A tool; ours answers coverage, hours and "what do you fix" before a visitor has to call.
- **Content/journal built out in full** — two competitors *reference* a blog but don't show it developed; ours ships with 6 complete Queensburgh/Durban-specific articles plus FAQ schema, ready to index.
- **Maps + Navigate wired in** — none of the three competitors were confirmed to have an embedded map; ours has both an embed and a one-tap "Navigate" link to the Queensburgh base.
- **Signature motion/brand craft** — the self-drawing wrench motif and cohesive charcoal/hi-vis-orange identity read as more considered than any of the three competitor sites, which lean on stock template layouts.

## Where competitors beat us today

- **Real job photography.** Mobile Mechanic Durban shows six real gallery photos of actual work; ours is disclosed stock. This is the single biggest credibility gap and the first thing to fix before launch.
- **Named testimonials.** Mobile Mechanic Durban displays three named customer quotes; we show only the aggregate 4.9★/73 rating as a claim, with a link out to Facebook rather than quotes on-page.
- **Structured enquiry intake.** Mobile Mechanic Durban and AC Auto Services both run proper booking/enquiry forms; we rely on WhatsApp and phone only.
- **Suburb-level coverage depth.** Mobile Mechanic Durban names 50+ specific suburbs; our Coverage section deliberately stays general rather than claim precision we haven't verified.

## The two sharpest gaps

1. **Real call-out photography** — the demo's own footer discloses it's stock. This is the fastest-noticed, highest-trust-cost gap next to a competitor with six real job photos.
2. **On-page social proof beyond the rating number** — competitors show either named testimonials or nothing; we show a number with no supporting quote, when the actual proof (73 reviews) lives on Facebook and isn't pulled onto the site.

## What closes the gap

1. **Real job photography** — swap in Johnny's own call-out photos at handoff; folded into the Foundation build, no separate rate-card line, but needs Johnny to supply or arrange a shoot.
2. **Pull real review quotes** — 3–5 real Google/Facebook review quotes (with permission) into the Reputation section; a light copywriting pass, R850/page if it needs a dedicated write-up.
3. **SEO Launch (R4,500 once)** — gets schema, sitemap and local business markup in place so the site can start competing for "mobile mechanic near me" once live; **SEO Visibility (R4,500 pm)** is the option to raise if Johnny wants to actively out-rank these three.
4. **DialCover AI call answering (R3,500 + R1,650 pm)** — none of the three competitors show an AI-answered line; catches calls missed while Johnny's mid-repair, which none of them appear to solve either.
5. **Extra suburb/area pages (R1,400 each)** — if Johnny wants to match Mobile Mechanic Durban's suburb-level depth later.
