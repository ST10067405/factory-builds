## Where we already win

- **Only site with a content/journal section** — 6 Durban-specific articles with FAQ schema. None of the three competitors publish any blog or ongoing content — this is Vawda's best organic-search lever once live.
- **Built-in FAQ chat widget** — none of the three competitors have any on-page Q&A tool; ours answers the top questions before a visitor has to call.
- **Signature motion/brand craft** — the animated pipe-divider and cohesive navy/orange identity read as more considered than any of the three competitor sites, which lean on stock WordPress-style templates or plain photo grids.
- **Lightweight, single-file build** — faster, simpler page than CMS-driven competitor sites (no plugin overhead).

## Where competitors beat us today

- **Real job photography.** All three competitors show authentic project photos; ours is disclosed stock. This is the single biggest credibility gap and the first thing to fix before launch.
- **Named, specific reviews.** ERS (4.9★/26 reviews, shown inline) and Dudley's (five named 5-star quotes) both display individual testimonials. We show only the aggregate 5.0/48 rating as a claim — no supporting quotes yet.
- **Page depth for local SEO.** ERS runs dedicated Areas and Projects pages covering 14+ suburbs — more indexable ground to rank on "plumber near me" style searches than our single page currently offers.
- **Structured lead intake.** Dudley's WhatsApp flow explicitly asks for photos, address and problem description up front — a smoother qualification step than a bare WhatsApp link.

## What closes the gap

1. **Real job photography** — swap in Vawda's own photos at handoff; folded into the Foundation build, no separate rate-card line, but needs the client to supply or arrange a shoot.
2. **Named review quotes** — pull 3–5 real Google review quotes (with permission) into the Reviews section; a light copywriting pass, R850/page if it needs a dedicated write-up.
3. **SEO Launch (R4,500 once)** — gets schema, sitemap and local business markup in place so the site can start competing with ERS's page-depth advantage; **SEO Visibility (R4,500 pm)** is the option to raise if Vawda wants to actively out-rank these three on "plumber near me" / "emergency plumber Durban."
4. **DialCover AI call answering (R3,500 + R1,650 pm)** — none of the three competitors offer AI-answered calls; this doesn't just close a gap, it leapfrogs all three on responsiveness for an emergency trade.
5. **Extra service/area pages (R1,400 each)** — if Vawda wants to match ERS's suburb-level depth later.
