Common questions

What services does Suren's Car Upholstery offer?

Seat re-upholstery, headliners, carpets, convertible and soft-top trim, leather repair and redye — in leather, vinyl or fabric.

Do you only work on cars?

Cars and bakkies are the bulk of the work, but the same trim skills cover golf carts, boats and bike seats too — ask if you're not sure.

How does a quote work?

Send a photo of the seat or trim on WhatsApp and you'll get a straight quote back — no need to bring the vehicle in first just to get a number.
