## Where competitors beat us today
- **Comp A and Comp B operate at a visibly bigger scale.** McCarthy's is a multi-city chain (Cape Town, Johannesburg, Durban) offering paint, ceramic coating and wheel restoration alongside trim; Jumbo's covers cars, marine, buses, aircraft and motorsport. Neither proves it with photos, but the breadth itself can read as more "established" to a first-time visitor than a single-location workshop with no site at all.

- **None of the three show real prices, bookings, or reviews either** — this isn't a gap so much as a wide-open field. Whoever adds real pricing, verified reviews, or before/after proof first takes a genuine lead in this catchment.

## The two sharpest gaps
1. **No proof of scale or process depth, vs Comp A and Comp C.** McCarthy's multi-service breadth and Handel Street's "custom, frame-retained" explanation both read as more considered than our current generic six-item service grid with "R —" pricing.
2. **No before/after photo pairs, vs the trade's own best-practice.** None of the four sites currently do this well, but for a trim/upholstery business specifically, a real before/after gallery is the single strongest trust signal available — and it's the one thing none of the three competitors have managed either.
