Common questions

Do I need a doctor’s referral to see a physio?

No — physiotherapists in South Africa are first-contact practitioners, so you can book directly. Some medical aids ask for a referral for claims purposes, so it’s worth checking your plan first.

Do you accept medical aid?

Medical aid claims — ask us. WhatsApp your scheme and membership details and we’ll confirm what’s claimable before you book.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Comfortable clothing you can move in, any recent scans or referral letters you have, and a list of current medication if relevant.

How do I book?
