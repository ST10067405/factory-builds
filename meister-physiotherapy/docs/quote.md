## Our recommendation between the two — The Structure, R32,000

The Foundation (R16,500) is our usual "demo goes live" recommendation, but this practice needs more than a brochure. The pain signal is specific: *a perfectly-reviewed practice (5.0★, 26 reviews) that patients can't check treatments or therapists for, and can only reach through one WhatsApp number since the practice email is reportedly down*. That's a booking-and-trust problem, not a content problem — which is what Structure is built for:

- **Booking form + lead routing** — gives patients a second, structured way to request an appointment beyond a single WhatsApp thread, closing the exact gap in the pain signal.
- **Practitioner/service pages + gallery/testimonials** — the demo currently uses generic role labels ("Principal Physiotherapist", "Physiotherapist") with no names, since none were supplied; Structure's practitioner pages are where real bios, photos and individual review quotes belong once the practice provides them.
- **Full on-page SEO + analytics/conversion tracking** — needed simply to start being findable at all; today the only public footprint is Google Business, Instagram and Facebook, no owned website.
- **Self-update journal** — already built into the demo; Structure is the tier that includes training so the practice can keep adding to it.
- ≤10 pages, 3 revisions + training.

## Worth adding

- **AI website chatbot — R9,500 setup + R950/mo.** The demo already proves the concept with a rules-based FAQ bot; upgrading it lets it handle real medical-aid and booking questions properly, including after-hours — useful given the practice currently has no working email as a backup channel.
