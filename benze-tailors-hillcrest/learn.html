The Cutting Table — Style & Care Journal

Practical notes on fit, alterations and getting suited up for Hillcrest's biggest occasions — from the team at Benze Tailors Hillcrest.

Common questions

What should I bring to my first fitting?

Wear or bring the shoes and shirt you'd pair with the finished garment, and bring any reference photos or an existing piece whose fit you like — it helps get the first measurements right.

Where is Benze Tailors Hillcrest?

134 Oxford Centre, 9 Old Main Rd, Hillcrest, Durban 3610 — inside the Oxford Centre on Old Main Road. Tap Navigate on the home page for directions.

Do you only make new suits, or do you alter existing ones too?

Both — made-to-measure suits alongside alterations & repairs, matric-dance and wedding wear, and restyling of suits you already own.

How do I book?
