## Our recommendation — The Foundation + SEO Launch, R21,000

**The Foundation (≤5 pages, motion, WhatsApp, Maps, on-page SEO, hosting + domain handled, 2 revisions) + SEO Launch setup (technical SEO, GBP, indexing, schema, tracking).**

Benze Tailors Hillcrest has real credibility working against it online: five decades of tailoring experience and a 5.0★/23-review Google rating, with **zero owned web presence**. Worse, anyone who searches "Benze" today can land on **benze.co.za — a completely different, unrelated business (upholstery), not this tailor.** That's not a nice-to-have gap, it's actively costing them the customers who go looking.

The Foundation gets a real site live with their own domain (something other than benze.co.za, which isn't available to them — see `needs-assessment.md`, section 08). SEO Launch is what makes that domain findable and correctly attributed to *this* Benze, not the other one — which is why we're recommending it as a paired once-off rather than a "maybe later" add-on. An ongoing Visibility retainer isn't justified yet (single location, no seasonal ad spend visible) — a proper Launch setup is the right first move.

The Structure (R32,000) isn't justified here — there's no booking-system depth, multi-location complexity, or e-commerce need for a single-shop, appointment-based tailor.
