## Where we're already ahead

- We're the only one of the four with a WhatsApp CTA and an on-site chatbot answering the obvious questions (location, hours, services, booking) without leaving the page — none of the three competitors have either.
- We're the only one with an embedded Google Map and a one-tap "Navigate" link — the other three either give an unconfirmed embed or plain text address.
- We're the only one with any content/journal system — 6 Hillcrest-specific tailoring articles plus FAQPage schema markup already in the code. None of the three competitors run a blog.
- We display the real, current Google rating (5.0★/23) directly and honestly, with no invented review quotes — only B Gangaram shows an equivalent trust signal (two testimonials), and it's static text, not a live rating.

## Where competitors beat us today

- All three are live, indexed, real websites today. Ours is a draft (`noindex`), not yet public — and critically, "Benze" as a search term currently resolves to **benze.co.za, an unrelated business**, which none of these three competitors have to contend with.
- Suit Yourself has real product photography; ours is 100% stock, clearly disclosed as such.
- B Gangaram displays real customer testimonials; we deliberately show none (no real quotes exist yet to use honestly).

## The two sharpest gaps

1. **Zero real photography.** The one photo Serper pulled for this build failed vetting (mismatched setting, likely a different business) and was rejected — every image on the demo is stock. For a bespoke tailor, finished-garment and fitting photos do more to build trust than almost any other feature, and it's the one thing none of our competitors do perfectly either — but Suit Yourself already beats us here with real product shots.
2. **Not live, not indexed, and name-collision risk.** The site is a `noindex` draft, and even once it launches, "benze.co.za" is already taken by an unrelated business — so simply going live isn't enough; the domain and search presence both need active, deliberate work to make sure customers find *this* Benze.

## What closes them

1. **Real photography** — client-supplied photos of finished garments and fittings, or a shoot arranged separately (no rate-card line for the shoot itself; swapping in supplied photos is part of the build).
2. **A domain other than benze.co.za, secured early** — precondition, not billable, but blocks everything below it.
3. **SEO Launch (R4,500 once-off)** — technical SEO, GBP, indexing and schema once the site is live under its own domain; this is the line item that actually closes gap #2, and it's why we're calling it out as unusually valuable for this client specifically (see `quote.md`).
4. **WhatsApp automation (R6,500 setup)** — turns our one already-clear lead (WhatsApp CTA) into a fully triaged booking channel, widening the gap over all three competitors rather than just matching them.
