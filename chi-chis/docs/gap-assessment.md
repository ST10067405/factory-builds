## Where we already win
- Only one of the four with a WhatsApp click-to-chat CTA — matches how clients actually message a salon.
- Only one with an on-site chatbot answering location/hours/booking instantly.
- Only one that publishes any content at all — a 6-article Journal with FAQ schema; none of the three competitors have a blog or journal.
- Lighter and faster than Cerése's JS-only shell and the two heavier WordPress/site-builder pages.
- Still surfaces Chi Chi's real 4.9★/83-review Google standing on-page — two of three competitors don't show any review signal at all.

## Where competitors beat us today
- Timeless Touch shows real treatment prices; ours are still placeholders pending Chi Chi's price list.
- Synergy Hair has a live online booking system; we're phone/WhatsApp-only.
- Synergy Hair has real, schema-marked client reviews and a dedicated gallery of genuine salon photos; our gallery is currently 100% stock (disclosed) and our reviews are one aggregate stat, not quotes.
- Synergy Hair carries full LocalBusiness structured data; our homepage has none yet (only the Journal page has FAQ schema).
- Our page is set to noindex while in draft — none of it is discoverable by Google yet (expected pre-launch, but a go-live task).

## What closes the gap
- **Booking integration (Fresha-style) — R3,600.** Matches Synergy Hair's live booking and closes the "no online booking" pain point directly.
- **Real photography for the gallery/hero** — replace the stock-only set with Chi Chi's actual studio and finished work (client-supplied photos or a short shoot). No separate rate-card line; a hard prerequisite before launch.
- **SEO Launch setup (R4,500 once)** — add LocalBusiness schema and full on-page meta to the homepage, then drop noindex at go-live.
- **Copywriting for the treatment menu, 5 pages — R3,500.** Turns "R —" placeholders into a real, confident menu once Chi Chi's shares pricing.
