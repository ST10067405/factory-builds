## Our recommendation between the two — The Foundation (R16,500)

Chi Chi's needs is contained: a treatment menu, a gallery, and a booking link — exactly the Foundation's scope (≤5 pages, motion, WhatsApp, Maps, basic SEO, hosting + domain handled, 2 revisions). This isn't a deep-booking or high-page-count business like a dental practice or a franchise, so the Structure tier's extra machinery (self-update journal engine, analytics/conversion tracking, 10-page depth) isn't needed yet — it's an easy upsell later once the site is proving itself.

## Worth adding

- **Booking integration — R3,600.** Salons live on bookings, Fresha-style. Every competitor we checked in this catchment already runs some form of online booking (see gap-assessment.md) — a booking link/widget closes the "no online booking" pain signal directly.
- **Copywriting, 5 pages — R3,500 (optional).** The treatment menu currently shows placeholder pricing ("R —"). Once Chi Chi's shares a real price list, proper copy turns that into a confident, sellable menu instead of a gap.

## Keeping it alive

- **Care plan: Essential (R450 pm)** by default — covers basic upkeep. Upgrade to **Professional (R1,200 pm)** if Chi Chi's wants the gallery refreshed monthly with new styles/work — plausible given how Instagram-active the business already is.
- **SEO Launch (R4,500 once-off)** — recommended for everyone at go-live: on-page basics, schema, submitting to Google. **Visibility (R4,500 pm)** is worth flagging as optional, not default: hair-salon competition in Westville/Upper Highway is real — we found three competitors with live, ranking sites in this exact catchment during research (see competitors.md). If Chi Chi's wants to actively chase local search rank against them, Visibility is the lever; if not, Launch alone is enough to be found.
