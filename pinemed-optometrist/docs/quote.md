## Our recommendation between the two — The Structure, R32,000

The Foundation (R16,500) is our usual "get a demo live" recommendation, but the pain signal here is specific: *a 5.0★ independent practice sitting a few doors down from Specsavers and Torga, with no way for anyone to find it, trust it, or book with it online.* That's a booking-and-trust problem against well-resourced franchise competitors, which is exactly what The Structure is built for:

- **Booking form + enquiry routing** — turns "call or WhatsApp only" into an actual online booking path, closing the exact gap that lets a franchise next door win on convenience alone.
- **Gallery/testimonials + service pages** — a 5.0★/24-review independent practice has real trust equity that a franchise chain can't easily replicate; Structure is built to surface it properly, not just state the number.
- **Full on-page SEO + indexing + analytics/conversion tracking** — matters specifically here because the practice is competing for the same local searches ("optometrist Pinetown", "eye test Pinetown CBD") that the franchises already rank for.
- **Self-update journal** — already built into the demo; Structure includes the training so the practice can keep adding to The Sightline itself.
- Up to 10 pages, 3 revisions + training.

## Worth adding

- **AI website chatbot — R9,500 setup + R950/mo (optional).** The demo's rules-based bot already covers the four obvious questions; upgrading to a real AI chatbot lets it handle anything a patient asks (lens types, aftercare, walk-in vs appointment) instead of four canned answers. Optional, not core.

## Keeping it alive

- **Care plan: Essential, R450/mo** (default). A single-practice, single-location site doesn't need frequent hands-on changes once services and hours are locked in — move to Professional (R1,200/mo) only if the practice wants us actively adding to the Journal or updating services monthly.
- **SEO: Launch setup, R4,500 once** — the baseline for any local business going live from zero.
- **SEO: Visibility, R4,500/mo** (min 3-month term) — recommended on top, because the competition here isn't just other independents, it's Specsavers and Torga, both of whom already have SEO-tagged, franchise-backed sites in the same shopping precinct. Going live without ongoing SEO means starting behind, permanently, against opponents with far bigger marketing budgets. (Visibility + GEO, R8,500/mo, is a stretch option if the practice later wants visibility in AI-generated answers — worth mentioning, not the lead recommendation.)
