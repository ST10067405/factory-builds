## Where we're ahead

- We're the only one of the four with a confirmed, working WhatsApp click-to-chat CTA — none of the three competitors show one in their page markup.
- We're the only one with any chatbot/instant-answer tool, even in rules-based demo form.
- We already have a 6-piece eye-health content hub ("The Sightline") with FAQPage schema — no evidence any of the three competitors publish ongoing content.
- We surface the real 5.0★/24-review rating directly on the page; none of the three competitors show an equivalent live rating widget.
- Our page is a single lightweight static build — Graham Lewis carries a JS-heavy Revolution Slider plugin, and Kloof Optometrists' full weight couldn't even be measured from a static fetch (client-rendered app).

## Where we're behind

- All three are live, indexed, real domains today; ours is `noindex` and draft — they outrank us from day one until we go live and start building authority.
- Westville Eyecare already has a "Book now" mechanism of some kind on its homepage; our demo still says "online booking is coming to this site soon."
- All three (where confirmable) likely have real practice photography; ours is 100% disclosed Unsplash stock, pending the client's own photos.
- Graham Lewis Optometrist has a Google Maps embed live today; we have one too, but theirs is already indexed and public.

## The two sharpest gaps

1. **No real booking mechanism, against a franchise-dense catchment where convenience wins by default.** Westville Eyecare already shows some form of "Book now"; the two franchises next door to PineMed (Specsavers, Torga) will have polished booking flows too.
2. **We're invisible to search while three competitors (plus both nearby franchises) are already indexed and live.** Going live from zero means starting behind on "optometrist Pinetown" and similar searches.

## What closes them

1. **Real booking mechanism** — The Structure (R32,000) includes the booking form + enquiry routing; add the R3,600 booking/calendar integration for genuinely live slots, matching or beating Westville Eyecare's "Book now" CTA.
2. **Go live and index immediately** — SEO Launch, R4,500 once, then Visibility, R4,500/mo, so the practice isn't starting from zero against three already-indexed independents and two well-resourced franchise chains in the same precinct.
3. **Swap stock for real photos** — no rate-card cost, just client action, once the practice supplies interior/frame/team photos.
4. **Turn the 5.0★/24 rating into individual testimonial quotes** — in scope under The Structure, once real review text is pulled and confirmed.
