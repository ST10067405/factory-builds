Plain-language reads from PineMed Optometrist, for Pinetown CBD readers who'd rather understand their eyes than guess.

Common questions

How often should I get an eye test?

As a general guideline, most adults benefit from an eye test every one to two years, and more often for children, contact lens wearers, or anyone with a family history of eye conditions. An optometrist can recommend a schedule that suits you specifically.

Do you accept medical aid?

Medical aid claims — ask us. WhatsApp or call 071 014 2046 and we'll help you check what your scheme covers.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Your current glasses or contact lenses (if you wear them), any previous prescription you have, your medical aid card, and a pair of sunglasses in case your eyes feel more light-sensitive afterwards.
