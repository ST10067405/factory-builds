Common questions

What's the difference between a learner's licence and a driving licence?

A learner's licence is a theory-based test on road signs, rules and vehicle controls — it doesn't let you drive alone yet. A driving (K53) licence comes after, and covers a yard test and an actual road test with an examiner.

How many lessons do I need before my test?

It depends on how much driving experience you already have — some learners are ready after a handful of focused lessons, others need more time to build confidence. MZO Legacy can advise once they've assessed your current skill level.

What should I bring to my K53 test?

Typically your ID document, your learner's licence (for the driving test), and the required fee — always confirm the exact requirements with your local testing station before test day, as these can change.
