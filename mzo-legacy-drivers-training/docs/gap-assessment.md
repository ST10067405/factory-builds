## Where we're ahead
- We're the only one of the four with an on-site chatbot answering common questions instantly.
- We're the only one with embedded Maps + directions — none of the three competitors have a map or navigate link on their homepage.
- We're the only site with schema markup on *both* pages (LocalBusiness on the homepage, FAQPage on the journal) — Apex has homepage schema too, but nothing on a content page.
- WhatsApp is presented as prominently for us as for the competitors that have it (hero, floating button, footer, all pre-filled) — matching Apex's and Ontrack's implementations rather than trailing them.

## Where competitors beat us today
- **Apex and Ontrack both show real photo galleries.** Ours is fully stock, honestly disclosed in the footer — no client photography exists yet for MZO Legacy.
- **Apex has real, written testimonials** and an active "Insights" blog with analytics tracking — the most digitally mature of the three, and the clearest sign that competitors in this space are already investing in content and proof.
- **Apex runs analytics/conversion tracking** — we have none yet (that's a Structure-tier feature, not included in Foundation).
- **All three competitors have a working WhatsApp link already live** — this isn't a gap in features, but it does mean WhatsApp-first booking is already the market norm here, not a differentiator on its own.

## The two sharpest gaps
1. **No real photos.** Every competitor with a gallery (Apex, Ontrack) shows actual lesson/instructor imagery; ours is stock-only. For a business built on trust ("is this school legit?"), real photos are the single highest-leverage fix once the client can supply them.
2. **No online booking.** All four sites — ours included — fall short of true calendar booking, but this is exactly the gap the "online booking is coming soon" chatbot hook is designed to close, and it's priced and ready to add (booking/calendar integration, R3,600).

## What closes the gap
- **Real photos** — not a rate-card line item; a prerequisite the client needs to supply before launch (see needs-assessment Notes).
- **Booking/calendar integration** (R3,600 once) — closes the booking gap directly, and would put MZO Legacy ahead of all three competitors on this feature.
- **SEO Launch** (R4,500 once) — brings schema/analytics basics in line with Apex, the strongest competitor.
- **Testimonials** — once real review quotes are sourced and verified from Google, replacing the aggregate-only rating strip would match Apex's testimonial section directly (no extra build cost — a copy/content task once quotes are supplied).
