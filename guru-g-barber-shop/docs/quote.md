## Our recommendation between the two: The Foundation — R16,500

Guru G is a single-location, walk-in barbershop — not a multi-branch office, a dentist, or a fine-dining venue that needs Structure-level booking/menu depth on day one. The Foundation covers exactly what's missing to go from demo to live: taking the site off `noindex`, formalising the WhatsApp/Maps work already prototyped, basic on-page SEO (currently only the Journal page carries schema), hosting + domain, and 2 rounds of revisions. It matches the painSignal directly — "busy walk-in barbershop, word of mouth only" needs a live, findable site before it needs a booking engine.

**Stretch flag — read this before quoting:** Guru G is not a single shop. The lead data lists branches at Westville, Kloof, Maytime and Westwood, and our own photo-sourcing turned up images mistakenly tagged to *three different* Guru G locations (Westwood Mall, Waterfall, Hillcrest Corner) plus a second Fresha listing at 119 Jan Hofmeyr Rd — a different Westville address entirely. If the real conversation is "build the group a presence," that's **The Structure (from R32,000)**: branch pages/finder, booking form + lead routing per branch, full on-page SEO across locations. Worth raising on the call before quoting Foundation as final — see `needs-assessment.md` §10.
