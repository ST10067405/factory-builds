Skills fade faster than certificates expire. These are short, practical reads on the things people ask Kate about most between courses. Worth five minutes now, so the knowledge is there when you need it.

Common questions

How long is a first aid certificate valid for?

KV First Aid certificates are issued on completion and are valid for up to three years, depending on the course. The expiry date is printed on the certificate itself. Diarise it when you file the certificate, and book the refresher a month or two before it lapses so your cover never has a gap.

Do you come to our workplace, or do we travel to you?

Either way. Kate travels to you for onsite courses anywhere from Pietermaritzburg through to Durban and the towns in between, so your team trains in the space they actually work in and you lose no time to travel. If it suits you better, she is based in Kloof and has a training room on the premises.

What is the difference between Level 1 and Level 2 first aid?

Level 1 covers basic life support and the essential first aid skills most workplaces need: CPR, choking, bleeding, burns and the recovery position. Level 2 builds on that with more advanced skills for a wider range of workplace and everyday emergencies.
