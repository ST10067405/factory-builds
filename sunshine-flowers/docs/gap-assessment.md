## Where we're ahead
- We're the only one of the four with an on-site chatbot answering common questions instantly.
- We're the only one with an embedded Google Map and a one-tap "Navigate" link — none of the three competitors show a map on-page.
- We're the only one with a content journal (6 flower-care/gifting articles + FAQ schema) — none of the three run a visible blog.
- We combine WhatsApp-first ordering with a real, disclosed occasions structure (birthdays, sympathy, weddings, corporate) in one page — Comp A is the only competitor with WhatsApp at all, and none present an occasions breakdown this way.

## Where we're behind
- **No real photos yet.** All three competitors show real product/storefront photography; our demo is openly stock, with a disclosure line, pending real photos from the client.
- **No visible prices.** Comp A and Comp C both publish full ZAR pricing; we show "R —" placeholders throughout, by house rule, until real numbers are supplied.
- **No online ordering/cart.** Comp A and Comp C both run full e-commerce (cart + checkout); Comp B links out to a Shopify store. We deliberately use WhatsApp-only ordering per the brief's angle — a real product/positioning choice, not an oversight, but it means we can't compete on "buy now" convenience without the optional light-ordering-path add-on.
- **No real review quotes.** Comp B displays three genuine 5-star testimonials; we show only the real aggregate 4.8★/20 rating, with no invented quotes.
- **No published hours.** Comp C states real opening hours (Mon–Sat); ours reads "to be confirmed — WhatsApp us" because no hours were supplied in the brief.

## The two sharpest gaps
1. **No real photos, vs. all three competitors.** Every comparable florist site we found shows real product or storefront photography; our demo is disclosed stock. This is the single most visible trust gap on a florist site, where the product *is* the photo.
2. **No prices and no online ordering, vs. Comp A and Comp C.** Two of the three run full e-commerce with real ZAR pricing; we show "R —" and a WhatsApp-only flow. That's a deliberate design choice for a one-person operation, but it means anyone comparison-shopping across these sites sees prices on two of three competitors and not on ours.

## What closes them
- **Real photos from the client** — not a rate-card line item, but the fastest, highest-impact fix; replaces the disclosed stock images with Sunshine Flowers' own arrangements and nursery.
- **Real pricing supplied by the client** — replaces every "R —" placeholder once the client is ready to publish numbers; no build cost, just client input.
- **Light ordering path** (payment gateway R3,200 + products page R1,400, priced in `quote.md`) — closes the "no cart" gap for standard, repeat orders while keeping bespoke arrangements on WhatsApp.
- **WhatsApp automation** (R6,500 setup, priced in `quote.md`) — doesn't add reviews or a cart, but directly strengthens our one real lead: every WhatsApp enquiry gets an instant response instead of waiting on a manual reply.
