Flower Care & Gifting Notes

Practical tips from a Pinetown florist — keeping blooms fresh in the heat, choosing flowers for the moments that matter, and getting the most out of every order.

Common questions

How long will my bouquet last?

It depends on the flowers, but fresh water changed every one to two days, a clean re-cut of the stems, and keeping the arrangement out of direct sun and heat will get the most out of any bouquet — especially in Pinetown's warmer months.

Do you deliver?

Delivery is available across Pinetown and surrounds — ask about your specific area when you WhatsApp your order.

Can I just send a photo of what I want?

Yes — a reference photo, a colour scheme, or even just a budget and occasion is enough to get started. We'll send back photo options before anything is finalised.

How do I book?
