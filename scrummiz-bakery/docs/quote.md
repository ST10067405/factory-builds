## Recommended path — The Foundation, R16,500

**The Foundation (up to 5 pages, signature motion, contact form + WhatsApp, Maps + GBP + basic SEO, hosting/domain/deploy handled, 2 revisions, 2–3 weeks).**

Scrummiz is a straightforward single-location bakery: one address, no franchise complexity, no e-commerce checkout needed for a made-to-order product. The pain signal is specific — a big Instagram following with an **abandoned, unpublished website** and no menu, pricing or order form anywhere online. We confirmed this directly: the old site (`scrummizbakery.company.site`, surfaced during competitor research) currently returns "the website is not available" — dead, no content, no phone number on it either. The Foundation's scope (home + journal, gallery, Maps embed, mobile-first build) already matches what this business needs; the two things that turn "concept" into "launchable" are (1) a real cake/macaron gallery and (2) a confirmed menu with real prices — every price on the demo currently reads "R —".

**One deviation to flag:** the Foundation package normally bundles WhatsApp as its default contact form. Scrummiz has no verified phone number anywhere in the pipeline, so this build substitutes Instagram DM as the interim contact channel throughout (nav CTA, floating action button, chat fallback). WhatsApp automation becomes available as a natural next step once a real business number exists — see "Worth adding" below.

The Structure (R32,000) isn't justified here — there's no booking-calendar depth, multi-service structure, or analytics need that a single-location made-to-order bakery requires.
