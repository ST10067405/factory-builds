## Where we're ahead

- We're the only one of the four with any content/journal system — 6 articles plus FAQPage schema markup already in the code. None of the three competitors run a blog.
- We're the only one with an on-site chat widget answering the obvious questions (location, what's baked, how to order, notice needed) without leaving the page.
- We're the only one with an embedded Google Map and directions link on the homepage — none of the three competitors show one.
- We display the real, current Google rating (5.0★/23) directly on the page; none of the three competitors show any review signal at all.

## Where we're behind

- All three have real photography of actual cakes, macarons or packaging. Ours is 100% stock, clearly disclosed as such.
- All three have a working instant-contact channel (WhatsApp or a chat button); we can't add WhatsApp yet — Scrummiz has no verified phone number, so we run Instagram DM instead.
- All three are live, indexed, real websites today. Ours is a draft (`noindex`), not yet public.
- CakesbyVan shows real, transparent pricing by category; ours shows "R —" throughout because there's no confirmed menu to work from yet.

## The two sharpest gaps

1. **No real photography.** Every competitor's site sells the work with real cake/macaron photos; ours is inspiration-only stock. This is the single biggest trust gap for a visual, made-to-order product.
2. **No phone number, so no WhatsApp.** All three competitors offer instant one-tap contact (WhatsApp or chat button); Scrummiz is DM-only until a real business number exists. This also blocks WhatsApp automation and any future DialCover call-answering.

## What closes them

1. **Real photography** — client-supplied cake/macaron photos (or an arranged shoot) to replace all 4 stock images. Bundled into the Foundation build; add copywriting (R850/page) if captions/descriptions are also wanted.
2. **A business phone number, then WhatsApp automation (R6,500 setup)** — the number itself is a precondition, not a billable line, but it's step one of closing the one gap all three competitors already have on us.
3. **A real, priced menu** — copywriting R850/page (or R3,500 for up to 5 pages) to replace the "R —" placeholders, once Scrummiz confirms flavours, sizes and prices.
4. **Visible pricing is a choice, not automatically a gap** — CakesbyVan shows price bands because their product is templated; Scrummiz's bespoke, DM-quoted model (matching Daniela's and Cake A Day, who also hide pricing) may be the right fit. Confirm with the client rather than assuming price transparency is wanted.
