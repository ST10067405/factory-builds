Celebration-baking notes, from Pinetown

Ordering know-how, macaron notes and cake-planning guidance from the Scrummiz Bakery counter on Sunnyside Lane.

Common questions

How far ahead should I order a custom cake?

It depends on the design and how busy the diary is — the safest approach is to DM us as soon as you have a date, even before every detail is final.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Ask us directly via DM — tell us what's needed and we'll confirm what's possible for your order.

How is cake size chosen for a celebration?

It's usually planned around your guest count and how many tiers you'd like — DM us your numbers and we'll suggest a starting size.

How do I order?

DM us on Instagram @scrummizbakery. Online ordering is coming to this site soon.
