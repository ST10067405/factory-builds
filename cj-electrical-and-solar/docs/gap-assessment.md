## Where we already win
- We're the only one of the four with an on-site chatbot answering common questions instantly.
- We're the only one with a real content journal *and* embedded Maps *and* WhatsApp all at once — the other two with content (JAK) or maps (Open Circuit) don't have both.
- Lightest, cleanest single-page load of the two content-forward sites (JAK is lighter, but has no chatbot, map, or reviews at all).
- WhatsApp is more prominent for us than any competitor — floating button, hero button, and footer, all pre-filled, versus a single WhatsApp link on the others.

## Where competitors beat us today
- **Open Circuit shows real prices** (budget bands via an interactive estimator) — we show "R —" placeholders. For a category (solar) where cost is the #1 objection, this is a real gap.
- **Open Circuit has live social proof** — an actual Google reviews widget with reviewer photos and a link straight to Google Maps reviews. We only state the aggregate rating as text.
- **Open Circuit's gallery is 50+ real project photos.** Ours is 4 stock images with a disclosed placeholder line — no client photography exists yet.
- **JAK has a genuine SEO-driven blog** targeting the exact same load-shedding/local-trade topics we picked for our journal, meaning that keyword territory already has a live competitor in it.
- **No homepage schema markup on our site** (FAQ schema only lives on the journal page) — Open Circuit and JAK both have structured data on their main page.

## What closes the gap
- **Real photos** — replace the stock gallery once the client supplies project photos; not a rate-card line item, but a prerequisite before launch (see needs-assessment Notes).
