about

A Studio Built for Focus

GraySculpt Pilates brings reformer-based training to Gillitts — a boutique studio designed for people who want to move well, build real strength, and feel calmer doing it.

Reformer-Led

Every class centres on the reformer — spring-loaded resistance that builds strength while working with your joints, not against them.

For Every Level

From first-timers to regulars, sessions are guided to match how your body's moving on the day.

A Considered Space

A calm, uncluttered studio in Gillitts Shopping Centre — built to help you switch off and focus for the hour.

Your First Class, Sorted

A few things that make a first reformer class easier.

01

What to Wear

Fitted, comfortable activewear — nothing too loose near the springs and straps.

02

Grip Socks

Reformer studios work in socked feet — grip socks are worth having on hand.

03

Arrive Early

A few minutes early gives time to get set up and settled before class starts.

04

Go At Your Pace

Reformer resistance adjusts to you — instruction is paced to how your body's moving that day.
