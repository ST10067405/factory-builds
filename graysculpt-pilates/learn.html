Reformer Pilates, Explained

Honest, practical guidance for anyone starting — or sticking with — reformer and mat Pilates in Gillitts.

Common questions

Do I need Pilates experience to start?

No — reformer classes are guided for your level, whether you're brand new or building on experience.

What should I wear and bring?

Comfortable, fitted activewear and grip socks (loose fabric can catch on the springs and straps). Bring water — mats are provided in-studio for mat sessions.

Where is the studio?

Shop 15, Gillitts Shopping Centre, 1 Clifton Rd, Gillitts, 3610.
