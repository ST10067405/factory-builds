## 2. Recommended path — The Foundation, R16,500

The Foundation (≤5 pages, motion, WhatsApp, Maps, basic SEO, hosting + domain handled, 2 revisions) is the right tier here, not the Structure. This is a single-operator mobile service with one core message — "we come to you, book by call or WhatsApp" — not a multi-location, multi-menu or high-transaction business that needs Structure-level depth (booking-form-with-lead-routing, full analytics stack, self-update journal). The demo already proves out a Foundation-shaped site; going live is mostly a matter of committing budget to make it real, not rebuilding it bigger.

**Why not Structure (R32,000):** nothing here needs 10 pages, e-commerce-grade lead routing or conversion-tracking depth — the stated pain is operational (bookings falling through the cracks), which the add-ons below solve directly, not extra pages.

## 3. Worth adding

- **Booking/calendar integration — R3,600.** The demo's own FAQ widget already admits "online booking is coming to this site soon" — there's no real booking mechanism today, only call/WhatsApp. Given the painSignal is bookings relying entirely on calls and referrals, a real calendar closes the actual gap, not just a cosmetic one.
- **WhatsApp automation — R6,500 setup.** This is the single highest-leverage add-on for this client: the whole business already runs on WhatsApp (it's the primary CTA on every button on the demo). The real pain isn't the website — it's double bookings, no-shows and voice-note chaos happening inside WhatsApp itself. Automating confirmations, reminders and structured intake directly addresses that, where a nicer homepage alone would not.
- **AI website chatbot — R9,500 setup + R950 pm (optional).** The demo already ships a free scripted FAQ widget covering the five most obvious questions. A real AI chatbot would let it handle anything a pet parent asks (not just the five canned ones) but isn't essential to fixing the booking-chaos problem — flagged as optional, not core.

## 4. Keeping it alive

- **Care plan: Essential — R450 pm.** This is a single-service, single-message site; content won't need frequent hands-on changes once the copy and services list are locked in with the client, so Essential (not Professional) is the right default.
- **SEO: Launch setup — R4,500 once.** Worth doing at minimum — our competitor check (see `competitors.md`) found at least one direct mobile-groomer competitor (Mobi Dog Grooma) already ranking organically for "mobile dog groomer Durban," so basic on-page SEO hygiene is not optional. A monthly Visibility retainer isn't recommended yet — better to stabilise the booking/WhatsApp operations first and revisit local-search spend once that's live.
