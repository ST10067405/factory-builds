## Our recommendation between the two

**The Foundation — R16,500 once-off.**

You already solved booking (Fresha) — that's the hard part most salons still don't have. What you're missing is discovery and brand: a site Google can find, that looks as good as the 4.6★ rating suggests, and that turns "well-rated salon" into "the one people call first." The Foundation (≤5 pages, motion, WhatsApp, Maps, basic SEO, hosting + domain handled, 2 revisions) takes this demo from concept to live, with your real photos, real hours, and real service menu in place. Structure (R32,000) would be overkill here — you don't need a native booking form or a self-service journal CMS; Fresha already handles bookings and your Journal content is fixed enough to ship as static pages.
