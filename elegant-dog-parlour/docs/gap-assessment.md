## Where we already win
- Only site of the four with both a WhatsApp click-to-chat button and an on-page FAQ chatbot — competitors either don't mention WhatsApp or only reference it in passing text (Comp C).
- Only site with real content marketing: a 6-article Journal plus FAQ schema. None of the three competitors run anything like a blog or guide content.
- Live Google rating (4.5★/121 reviews) surfaced directly in the hero and in schema markup — competitors leave this sitting in their separate Google listing rather than on-site.
- Built as a single lightweight static page — no WordPress/plugin overhead, versus all three competitors running WordPress.
- Honest about stock imagery instead of misrepresenting it — a small trust move most small-business sites don't bother with either way.

## Where competitors beat us today
- Hillcrest Dog Spa shows real, transparent prices by dog size — our demo still has placeholder "R —" pricing, a real gap for a phone-first customer trying to budget before calling.
- All three competitors are live, indexable sites today — ours is still a private draft (`noindex`, not deployed), so they outrank us by default simply by existing.
- Pets Parlour and Village Vet both have an actual photo gallery (content unverified, but presumably real work) — ours is disclosed stock, honest but not yet a selling point.
- Hillcrest Dog Spa has a "BOOK NOW" CTA — even unconfirmed as a full booking system, it signals more booking intent than our call/WhatsApp-only approach.

## What closes the gap
1. Get the client's real price list into the site — copywriting, R850/page (or R3,500 for 5 pages) — turns the biggest single trust gap into a strength, since only one competitor shows prices at all.
2. Go live — The Foundation, R16,500 — until this ships, competitors win on visibility purely by being indexed; nothing else here matters until it happens.
3. Real photography — flagged as a next step, not yet rate-carded — replaces disclosed stock with actual grooming-day photos, closing the gallery gap and reinforcing the 30-year trust story.
4. Booking/calendar integration — R3,600 — matches and beats Hillcrest Dog Spa's "BOOK NOW" CTA with an actual working booking flow instead of just a label.
