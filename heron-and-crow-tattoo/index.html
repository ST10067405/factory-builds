about

Two Artists, One Studio.

Heron & Crow Tattoo is a two-artist studio tucked into the Gillitts Shopping Centre on Clifton Road — Upper Highway's newest stop for considered, custom ink. Every piece starts as a conversation, not a flash-sheet pick, and it's built by hand around your idea, not pulled off a wall.

Consultation First

No walk-in guesswork — every custom piece starts with a real sit-down to talk placement, scale and style before a single line goes down.

Two Distinct Hands

Gillitts, Upper Highway

what we do

Styles We Work In.

A general guide to the ground our two resident artists cover between them — final fit is agreed at consultation.

01

Fine-Line

Delicate single-needle work — botanical detail, script and minimalist linework that ages best when it's done right the first time.

02

Blackwork

Bold saturated linework and fills — geometric patterns, mandalas and heavy black pieces built to hold their edge for years.

03

Colour Realism

Rich, considered colour work — portraits, florals and full-colour custom pieces planned around how skin actually holds pigment.

04

Cover-Ups

Honest reworks of old or regretted ink — a proper look at what's actually possible before any new line goes down.

Every piece is quoted individually after consultation — no fixed price list, no guesswork.

how it works

The Process.

01

Consult

Message us on WhatsApp with your idea, size and placement. We set up a proper sit-down before anything is booked.

02

Design

Your resident artist sketches and refines the piece around your body — style, scale and placement agreed before ink meets skin.

03

Session

Tattoo day. Sterile setup, one artist, your full session booked start to finish — no rushing a custom piece.

04

Aftercare

You leave with a full aftercare walkthrough and an open line on WhatsApp if anything about the healing looks off.
