## Our recommendation between the two — The Foundation, R16,500

**The Foundation (up to 5 pages, signature motion, contact form + WhatsApp, Maps + GBP + basic SEO, hosting/domain/deploy handled, 2 revisions).**

Heron & Crow is a straightforward single-location studio — two artists, one shop, no franchise complexity, no e-commerce. The demo already proves out a Foundation-scope build (home + journal, WhatsApp wired to a real number, Maps embed, mobile-first). The gap the package needs to close first is **real photography** — every image on the demo today is representative stock, because both pulled Google/social photos failed vetting (see `images/_assets-manifest.json`). For a portfolio-led tattoo studio, real session photos from the two resident artists are the single highest-leverage thing to add before this goes live — higher priority even than the add-ons below.

The Structure (R32,000) isn't justified here — there's no multi-artist booking-calendar depth, no self-update journal CMS need, and no analytics/conversion-tracking requirement for a two-chair studio running on Instagram and WhatsApp.

## Worth adding

- **Booking integration — R3,600.** Right now the site's "book" flow is WhatsApp only (an honest reflection of how the studio runs today). Nearby competitors are starting to move to platform booking — Sin on Skin (Westville) already runs a live "Bookings" + "Pricing" flow on their site. Wiring in a booking platform (Fresha is the local norm — Azalea Ink and Step Up Tattoo both use it) would close that gap if the studio wants self-serve scheduling. Optional: many boutique studios (Wild Orchid, Westville) deliberately stay consultation-gated instead — worth asking which model the two artists prefer before spending on this.
- **Photo/video shoot — TBC.** Not a fixed rate-card line. With two active resident artists and an Instagram following already, the fastest path may be sourcing existing session photography directly from the artists rather than commissioning a new shoot — flag this as the first client-input conversation, not a line item to quote blind.
- **Copywriting — R850/page.** If the two artists can't supply their own bios/style descriptions in writing, this covers turning what they tell us into finished copy for an artist-galleries structure.

## Keeping it alive

- **Care plan: Essential, R450/pm.** A two-chair studio with occasional style/gallery updates doesn't need Professional's 2-hour monthly change allowance — Essential (hosting, SSL, backups, uptime, patching, 30 min changes) covers routine upkeep without over-selling.
- **SEO: Launch setup, R4,500 once-off.** Worth doing once to get found for "tattoo studio Gillitts" / "tattoo Upper Highway" — real, live competitors already rank (Step Up Tattoo, Sin on Skin, Wild Orchid Tattoos — see `competitors.md`), but there isn't the search-intensity in this category to justify an ongoing Visibility retainer yet. Once-off Launch is the right size for now.
