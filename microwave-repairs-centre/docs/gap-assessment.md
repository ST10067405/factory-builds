## Where we already win
- We're the only one of the four with an on-site chatbot answering common questions instantly.
- We're the only one with real photography of actual repair work — all three competitors show either a logo only, no gallery, or a single generic clip-art appliance image. Our 4 workshop photos, modest as they are, are a genuine differentiator.
- We're the only one combining a content journal, embedded Maps, and prominent WhatsApp (hero + floating button + footer + in-chat) all at once.
- Our "since 2002 / 24 years" story is backed by a live, animated on-page callout — Comp C states 24 years too, but as plain text with no equivalent presentation.

## Where competitors beat us today
- **Comp A shows real prices.** A cost-range table (e.g. "Fridge repairs Westville: R450 – R2,500+") directly answers the #1 objection for a repair-vs-replace decision — we show "R —" placeholders. This is the single sharpest gap.
- **Comp A has a real booking form and an 8-step "how it works" explainer.** We only have a chat-answer promising booking "coming soon" — a live lead-capture form is a meaningful conversion edge they have and we don't yet.
- **Comp C has far deeper review social proof.** 125 Google reviews at the same 4.6★ rating as us (45 reviews) — nearly 3× our review volume, live-pulled testimonial text rather than a static number.
- **Comp B and Comp C both run multiple suburb-specific SEO pages** (Westville, Durban North, Umhlanga, Ballito, etc.) — we currently have one homepage and one journal page's worth of on-page SEO surface area.

## The two sharpest gaps
1. **No visible prices and no booking capture, vs Comp A.** Comp A publishes a real cost-range table and a working booking form; we show "R —" placeholders and a chat answer that says booking is "coming soon."
2. **Thinner review depth, vs Comp C.** Comp C pulls through 125 live Google reviews at the same 4.6★ rating we have (45 reviews) — nearly 3× the social proof, with testimonial text visible, not just a number.

## What closes the gap
- **DialCover AI call answering** (R3,500 + R1,650 pm) — turns "no booking form yet" into a moot point by making sure every missed call on a job still gets answered and captured as a lead, which none of the three competitors' phone-only or form-only setups guarantee either.
- **SEO Launch + Visibility** (R4,500 once + R4,500 pm) — closes the schema/keyword-volume gap directly; needed to compete with Comp A's and Comp B's active SEO investment, and to eventually support suburb-specific landing pages the way Comp B and Comp C already do.
- **More real photos from the client** — not a rate-card line item, but the fastest way to widen our existing photography lead over all three competitors (see needs-assessment Notes).
- **Real Google reviews (quotes)** — once the client is comfortable sharing a few, adding 2–3 real testimonial quotes (never invented) would help close the trust gap with Comp C's 125-review-strong social proof.
