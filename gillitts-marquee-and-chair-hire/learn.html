Planning notes for the Upper Highway

General, practical guidance on sizing, weather, timelines and hiring the right marquee for a wedding or function in Gillitts and the Upper Highway.

Common questions

How far in advance should I book a marquee?

Popular wedding weekends in spring and summer can book up months ahead across the Upper Highway. As a general guide, aim for at least 2–3 months' notice where you can, and earlier for peak-season dates.

What's included in a marquee hire?

Marquee hire on its own usually covers just the structure — tables, chairs, linen, draping, lighting and a dance floor are typically quoted separately so you can build the exact package you need.

Do you deliver and set up?

Yes — delivery, setup and breakdown are part of the service. Confirm site access, timing and any venue or council requirements when you get your quote.
