## Where we're ahead

- We're the only one of the four with an embedded Google Map and directions link on the homepage.
- We're the only one with any chatbot/FAQ widget answering common questions inline.
- We're the only one with a content/journal system — 6 articles plus FAQPage schema markup already in the code. None of the three competitors run a blog.
- We display the real, current Google rating (4.9★/80) directly on the page with schema markup; none of the three competitors show any reviews at all.
- WhatsApp is a sticky, one-tap CTA tied to a real number; only Buddies offers WhatsApp (via live chat), and neither Wyebank nor Dezigner show it at all.

## Where competitors beat us today

- Wyebank and Dezigner both show real event photography; ours is one real photo out of three gallery images, with the rest honestly disclosed as stock.
- All three are live, indexed, real websites today. Ours is a draft (`noindex`), not yet public — which is precisely the "directory stub only" problem this build needs to solve.
- Dezigner has a dedicated "Get quote" form as a second enquiry channel alongside phone/email; we currently route everything through WhatsApp only (a deliberate choice, since that's the client's real, working channel — but worth knowing it's not the only pattern in this market).
- Buddies leads with 30 years of trading history and B-BBEE certification as trust signals beyond reviews — a reminder that "15 years" is a real asset for us too, and should be as visible on the final site as it is on the demo.

## The two sharpest gaps

1. **Real photography.** Only one usable real photo was found; the other two gallery images are disclosed stock. Wyebank and Dezigner both show their actual work — we need to match that before launch.
2. **Being live and indexed.** Every competitor here is a real, findable website today. Ours is a `noindex` concept draft — which is the exact "wedding planners find only directory stubs" problem this project exists to fix.

## What closes them

1. **Real photography** — client-supplied photos of actual marquees, table styling and past setups, or an arranged shoot (photo/video shoot, TBC on the rate card). Bundled into The Foundation build once supplied; add copywriting (R850/page) if captions/descriptions are also needed.
2. **Going live** — The Foundation (R16,500) includes hosting, domain and deployment; SEO Launch (R4,500 once-off) covers technical SEO, GBP and indexing so the site actually surfaces for "marquee hire Gillitts" and similar Upper Highway searches instead of leaving wedding planners with only a directory stub.
3. **A dedicated quote form** — worth a mention, not a default recommendation: Dezigner runs one alongside WhatsApp. Given this client already has a real, working WhatsApp number, WhatsApp-first is the right call for now; a form could be added later without disrupting that.
