## Where we already win
- Only one of the four with a live chatbot of any kind (theirs is zero, ours is a starting point).
- Only one that puts a real Google rating (4.6/30) front and centre on-site — Matusadona and Hillview show none at all.
- Only one with a Journal built on structured FAQ schema out of the gate — none of the three competitors have any blog/journal content.
- Two of three competitors route booking through an external Nightsbridge widget with no on-page form; the third has no visible booking mechanism at all beyond a phone/reservation prompt. None have a real on-site booking form with lead routing — closing that gap (Structure tier) would make Villa Verde the first in this catchment with one.

## Where competitors beat us today
- Hillview publishes real room rates; we currently show none — defensible while rates are being confirmed, but a real diner-style objection once live.
- Matusadona has a live, working WhatsApp channel; ours is unconfirmed on the number we have — this needs resolving before go-live, not just before launch copy.
- All three appear to have larger, more established photo galleries than our 7 real images, and Fever Tree/Hillview both carry a proper meta description; our homepage is still `noindex` as a draft with LodgingBusiness schema but no Journal-page schema equivalent to Fever Tree's.
- "Villa Verde" is not a unique name — an unrelated guest house trading under the identical name surfaced during our own photo research (see needs-assessment.md notes). None of the three named competitors have this specific naming-collision problem to the same degree.

## The two sharpest gaps
1. **No real booking mechanism vs. two of three competitors already routing through an external widget.** We're currently behind even a bolted-on Nightsbridge iframe, let alone a proper on-site form.
2. **Unconfirmed WhatsApp + no room rates**, together, meaning a guest currently has no fast, real way to check price or reserve without a phone call.

## What closes them
- Real booking/calendar integration → **R3,600** (gets us past "message us" to an actual reservation flow — ahead of Fever Tree/Matusadona's bolted-on widgets, not just level with them).
- Confirm WhatsApp on 073 233 3033, or supply a confirmed number → client input, no cost — resolves the single biggest trust gap against Matusadona's live WhatsApp channel.
- Real room rates from the client → client input, no cost — closes the gap against Hillview directly.
- SEO Launch + Visibility+GEO → **R4,500 once + R8,500 pm** (adds indexable schema, removes `noindex`, and targets "farm stay near Durban"/"Assagay guest house" — the local-specific terms that matter given the name-collision risk noted above).
