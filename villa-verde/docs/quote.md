## Recommended path — The Structure (R32,000 once-off)

We're recommending **The Structure** rather than the entry-level Foundation, because Villa Verde's actual problem — guests only finding rooms via OTA/booking-site listings that take a commission — is exactly what Structure is built to fix:

- **No direct booking flow exists today.** A phone number and a "message us" link are the whole channel. Foundation gets you a contact form; Structure gets you a real booking form with enquiry routing and lead capture — the mechanism that actually shifts bookings away from commission-taking OTAs.
- **Rooms, rates and availability aren't static.** A guest house's room list and seasonal rates change — Structure's self-update journal and gallery/service pages mean you can keep that current yourselves, not wait on us for every edit.
- **The café and gallery are a real differentiator worth showing off properly**, not squeezing onto a single brochure page — Structure's page depth (up to 10) gives rooms, the café and the gallery each proper room to breathe, plus full on-page SEO and analytics to track whether the direct-booking push is working.

The Structure includes: ≤10 pages, the self-update journal, a booking form with lead routing, gallery/testimonials, full on-page SEO, analytics + conversion tracking, 3 revisions and training.
