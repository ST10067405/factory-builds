THE FLOOR

Straightforward training advice for the free weights, machines and cardio you'll find at Body Physique Gym in Escombe — no gimmicks, just the basics done right.

Common questions

Do I need to be experienced to train here?

No — free weights, machines and cardio suit both first-timers and experienced lifters. If you're new, start light and focus on form before adding weight.

What should I bring to my first session?

Comfortable training gear, closed shoes, and a water bottle. A small towel for equipment is good gym etiquette too.

Can I just do cardio, or is it a bodybuilding-only gym?

The floor covers free weights, machines and cardio, so you can build a programme around any goal — strength, conditioning or general fitness.
