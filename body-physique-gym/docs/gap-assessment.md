## Where we're already ahead

- We're the only one of the four with an on-site chat widget answering the obvious questions (location, hours, what's on the floor, how to join) without leaving the page.
- We're the only one with any content/journal system — 6 articles plus FAQPage schema markup already in the code. None of the three competitors run a blog.
- We're the only one with an embedded Google Map and directions link on the homepage — none of the three were found to have this.
- We display the real, current Google rating (4.9★/103) directly on the page as a standing badge; only Triple S has an equivalent live social-proof element (two hand-picked testimonials, not a rating count).

## Where competitors beat us today

- Access Gym publishes real membership pricing on its homepage; every price on our demo still reads "R —."
- All three are live, indexed, real websites today; ours is a draft (`noindex`), not yet public.
- Two of three (Access Gym, Pure Fitness) show some or fully real facility photography; ours is mostly stock (1 of 5 images genuinely Body Physique's own), clearly disclosed.
- Triple S Fitness displays testimonials and satisfaction metrics; we have a rating badge but no individual review quotes to show.

## The two sharpest gaps

1. **No published pricing.** Access Gym — a gym in the neighbouring suburb — already shows real day-pass and monthly numbers. Every price on our demo is a placeholder until Body Physique confirms membership rates.
2. **The site isn't live or indexed.** All three competitors are real, findable websites; ours is a noindex concept draft. This is also the single biggest opportunity: none of the three competitors have a chatbot, an embedded map, or a content journal — going live converts our current feature lead into an actual visibility lead.

## What closes them

1. **Confirmed membership pricing** — copywriting (R850/page) if Body Physique can't supply it directly; turns the "R —" placeholders into a real Memberships section.
2. **Going live** — The Foundation (R16,500) plus SEO Launch (R4,500) gets the site indexed and found for "gym Escombe" / "gym Queensburgh" searches, where franchise gyms currently dominate.
3. **WhatsApp automation (R6,500 once-off)** — formalises the "Join Now" flow on the channel that's already Body Physique's real, live contact method.
4. **Real photography** — client-supplied or arranged separately (no rate-card line); closes the gap with Access Gym and Pure Fitness's real facility imagery.
5. **Ongoing care (Essential, R450/pm)** — keeps hours, pricing and any changes current once they're confirmed, so the site doesn't drift back into "outdated" the way the competitors' thin sites already have (no blog, no fresh content on any of the three).
