## Where we're ahead
- We're the only one of the four with an on-site chatbot answering common questions instantly.
- We're the only one with an embedded Google Map and one-tap Navigate link — none of the three competitors show a map.
- We're the only one combining a content journal, WhatsApp (hero + floating button + footer + in-chat), and a chatbot all at once — Comp C comes closest with WhatsApp-first CTAs but has no journal, map, or chatbot.
- Our aggregate 4.8★/141-review rating is stronger than Comp C's stated 5.0★ off ~100 vehicles once review depth is weighed — 141 real Google reviews is a meaningful, verifiable number.

## Where we're behind
- **Comp A and Comp C both show real prices.** Comp A publishes a full tiered wash-package price list; Comp C publishes four package prices outright. We show "R —" placeholders throughout — the single sharpest gap for a comparison-shopping visitor.
- **Comp B and Comp C both show real social proof beyond a star rating** — named customer testimonials, and Comp B has an actual photo gallery of wash/valet work. We show only the aggregate 4.8/141 number (real, but no quotes or gallery depth) plus one authentic photo and disclosed stock fill.
- **Comp C's WhatsApp-first booking flow** ("book in 60 seconds") is a slicker lead-capture moment than our current WhatsApp FAB, even though it isn't a true calendar system.

## The two sharpest gaps
1. **No visible prices, vs Comp A and Comp C.** Both publish real price lists; we show "R —" placeholders throughout. This is the first thing a comparison-shopping visitor checks.
2. **Thin social-proof depth, vs Comp B and Comp C.** Both show named testimonials (Comp B also has a real work gallery); we currently have only the aggregate rating and one authentic photo — a real but shallow trust signal by comparison.

## What closes them
- **Real pricing from the client** — not a rate-card line item, but the fastest, highest-impact fix: once Queensburgh Car Wash confirms their actual wash prices, they replace every "R —" on the site immediately.
- **More real photos + a few real review quotes from the client** — closes the social-proof gap directly; per house rule we never invent testimonials, so this depends on the client sharing 2–3 real Google reviews and more wash-bay photos (see needs-assessment Notes).
- **WhatsApp automation — R6,500 setup** — turns our existing WhatsApp FAB into an auto-replying, lead-capturing flow that matches or beats Comp C's "book in 60 seconds" pitch.
- **SEO Launch — R4,500 once** — none of the three competitors show strong schema/SEO investment either, so this is a genuine opening: proper schema, indexing and GBP alignment would put Queensburgh Car Wash ahead of all three on findability, not just parity.
