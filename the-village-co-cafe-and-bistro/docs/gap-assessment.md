## Where we already win

- Only one of the four with a working FAQ chat widget answering location/menu/hours/ordering
  questions on the spot — none of the three competitors have any chat layer.
- A 6-post Journal with an FAQPage schema block already coded in, ready on day one — matching
  or beating all three competitors' blog depth without years of backlog.
- An embedded Google Map with a one-tap "Navigate there" link — Glenwood Bakery doesn't even
  have an embedded map, just a text address.
- Lighter and faster than Boston Brew's ~965KB Wix build — a real mobile-load advantage.
- Numeric social proof (4.5★, 60 reviews) surfaced right in the hero, not buried in a linked
  directory listing.

## Where competitors beat us today

- All three show real photos of their actual space/food (25+ each); our demo is stock-only,
  clearly disclosed on-page, until The Village Co sends real photos.
- Boston Brew and Humble Coffee both show real menu prices; ours is placeholder pricing ("R —")
  pending the client's sign-off.
- Humble Coffee runs a full online shop (cart, merch, gift vouchers) — genuine e-commerce depth
  we don't have.
- Glenwood Bakery has a working WhatsApp contact point; we can't add one yet because The
  Village Co has no phone/WhatsApp number on record.
- Humble Coffee's on-page SEO is stronger right now (full meta description, clean title); ours
  is deliberately noindexed as a pre-launch concept draft.
